About Me

The person behind the models

A quick intro to how I work and what I care about.

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer with a Computer Science background from Assiut National University. My focus is applied AI — not notebooks that stay in notebooks, but systems that actually run, respond in real time, and hold up on real data.

I've built applied AI, business intelligence, and software systems: a computer-vision face-recognition pipeline running at 30 FPS with 512-dimensional embeddings, an interactive Netflix BI analytics dashboard analyzing 8,800+ titles, a bank churn predictor trained on 10,000 customer records achieving 88% accuracy with a 0.86 AUC. Alongside modelling, I care about the engineering details — latency, serialization, reproducible pipelines and clean evaluation.

I'm currently training at the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI), going through business-oriented ML case studies with CIB and the DEPI program, and I mentor trainees through Python and Machine Learning as a certified mentor.
